# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that makes this project work:

- Being welcoming to people whose first language is not the one the thread is in. This project is
  written in two languages precisely because that matters.
- Giving and accepting technical criticism gracefully. "This is wrong because X" is a gift;
  "this is wrong" with no X is noise.
- Saying "I could not test this on hardware" instead of implying you did.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private; personal or political attacks; insulting or
  derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every space of the project — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through GitHub: open a private security advisory
on this repository, which is the only private channel available here, or contact
[@ffapd1989](https://github.com/ffapd1989) directly. Reports are handled discreetly, and the
person who reported will get an answer.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may ban
temporarily or permanently anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate. There is no appeals
committee: this is a one-person project, and the maintainer is the last word.
